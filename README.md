**Purple Evil** is a pixel-art fantasy roguelike where you must explore a dark ancient dungeon, fight hordes of monsters, defeat powerful bosses, and uncover the mystery of the mysterious purple energy that is consuming the world.

## Story & Lore
The story takes place in a world where everything is turned upside down: **knights fight with staffs, and mages fight with swords**.

Many centuries ago, the purple energy was sealed away in the depths of an ancient dungeon. But the seal has weakened, and the ancient entity is awakening once again. The academy mentor warns the students of the impending danger. The two main heroes—a swordsman and a mage—decide to enter the blue portal and venture into the dungeon to uncover the secret of the purple energy and defeat it.

## Key Features

*   **Two heroes, one mind:** You play as both the mage and the knight simultaneously. You can switch characters at any time (key **R**), but due to a delay, you won't be able to do it "on the run."
*   **Shared health:** Both characters share a single health pool. If one dies, the current level ends.
*   **Unique abilities:**
    *   Both heroes can perform a **dash** (F) with a cooldown.
    *   The mage has a unique ability—the **stunning dash** (X). It is faster than a normal dash and stuns a mob or boss for 10 seconds, but it has a much longer cooldown. Use it wisely!
*   **Death and saves:** After death, you can either start a new game from scratch or load from your last save (in the lobby before the level where you died).

## Dungeon Structure

The dungeon consists of **9 levels** divided into **3 stages**. After every two normal levels, you will face a boss. Mobs from previous stages can also appear in the next ones.

| Stage | Regular Enemies | Boss |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Stage 1** | Slimes of various colors, bat | Ancient Slime (Slime Boss) |
| **Stage 2** | Regular skeleton, assassin skeleton, skeleton knight | Skeleton Lich |
| **Stage 3** | Flying demon, demon crossbowman | Purple Lord (Demon) |

## Lobby & Shop

Between stages, the player enters a safe lobby. Here you can restore your strength before the next fight and purchase upgrades in the shop (opens with **B**).

**Available upgrades (6):**
1. Attack Speed
2. Max Health
3. Poison
4. Regeneration
5. Speed
6. Strength

⚠️ *Warning: With each new purchase, the price of the same upgrade doubles. Plan your spending carefully!*

| Dash | **F** |

| Shop (lobby only) | **B** |
